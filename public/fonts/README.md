CAL serves its fonts locally through `next/font/local`, so builds do not require a Google Fonts connection.

These unmodified Latin WOFF2 subsets are the Google Fonts assets previously downloaded by Next.js for this project:

- DM Serif Display, regular 400 — `dm-serif-display-latin.woff2`
- Inter, variable 100–900 — `inter-latin.woff2`
- JetBrains Mono, variable 100–800 — `jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2`

Each family is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The corresponding copyright notices and license texts are included alongside the font files, sourced from the `ofl/dmserifdisplay`, `ofl/inter`, and `ofl/jetbrainsmono` directories of https://github.com/google/fonts.
